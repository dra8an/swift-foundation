## Page structure

moonsighting.com (read 2026-11-05 with plain curl; robots.txt allows everything). Example URLs: https://www.moonsighting.com/1438rmd.html (country table filled in) and https://www.moonsighting.com/1447rmd.html (country table still a template). - Index: the home page https://www.moonsighting.com/ has a 'Monthly Moonsighting Reports' menu. The visible part shows 1445-1448 AH. The markup links every month of 1438-1448 AH. - URL pattern: one static HTML page per Hijri month, https://www.moonsighting.com/{AH year}{code}.html. The codes for months 1-12 are muh, sfr, rba, rbt, jmo, jmt, rjb, shb, rmd, shw, zqd, zhj (e.g. 1447rmd.html is 9/1447). - Year coverage, checked by HEAD requests on the Ramadan page: 1429-1436 and 1438-1448 answer 200. 1437 (rmd, muh, shw, zhj), 1418-1428 and 1449 answer 404. - Page content: hand-written HTML with uppercase tags; no API, CSV or JSON. Each page has a title 'Moonsighting for <Month> <year>'. It then has, in order:   - an 'Al urjoonul qadeem' (old/waning crescent) section;   - the conjunction time in UT and a prose visibility forecast;   - visibility-curve GIF maps (pattern visibilitycurves/{year}{code}_{m-d-yyyy}.gif, plus visibilitycurves/conjunction/conjunction{year}{code}.jpg);   - the 'earliest reported sighting';   - sighting reports grouped by Gregorian evening, as prose list items like 'Seen:', 'Not Seen:' or 'Announced:' from MCW members, often relaying official national announcements;   - for some months, an HTML table '1st Day of <Month> in Different Countries'. It has one row per country/community (about 86-118 rows) and two columns: (1) a date cell, text 'Month DD, YYYY (Weekday)', or '????' when not yet filled; (2) the country plus the rule it follows, e.g. 'Albania (Follow Saudi)', 'Bangladesh (Official Announcement)', 'Brunei (Local Sighting)', 'Canada (FCNA/ISNA ...)', and for Egypt and Turkey a short criterion label. - Coverage of the country table, by a rough markup count that may miss tables in other markup:   - it appears mainly on Ramadan, Shawwal and Dhu al-Hijjah pages;   - it is largely filled for 1438 rmd/zhj, 1441 rmd/shw/zhj, 1442 shw/zhj, 1443 shw/zhj and 1444 shw (1442 rmd and 1444 shw only partly);   - for 1445-1448, the pages checked show a 118-row template with '????' in nearly every date cell;   - most other month pages (e.g. 1447muh, 1448muh, 1448jmo, about 3 KB each) only have the conjunction and visibility text and no per-country table. - Older pages (e.g. 1430rmd) are long prose with country announcements inside the sighting reports and no table. ICOP: not obtained (site disallows Claude agents). See not_found.

## Verification

An independent verifier re-read the cited sources: **23 checks, 14 confirmed, 5 refuted, 4 unverifiable**; 0 dates re-checked, 0 wrong.

### Refuted (corrected or dropped)
- The moonsighting.com contact email is hidden behind Cloudflare email-protection → The general contact address can be read in the home page source as email@moonsighting.com (assembled by script, not Cloudflare-protected). Only the book-manuscript address is behind Cloudflare email-protection.
- Country table: heading '1st Day of <Month> in Different Countries', about 86-118 rows, two columns, date cell text 'Month DD, YYYY (Weekday)' or '????' → The date cell has three formats: 'Month DD, YYYY (Weekday)' (1438-1439), 'Month DD, YYYY' (1441-1442) and 'Month DD' with no year (1443-1444). The year must come from the page. Tables have about 113-123 rows, and the heading wording varies.
- The table is largely filled for 1438 rmd/zhj, 1441 rmd/shw/zhj, 1442 shw/zhj, 1443 shw/zhj and 1444 shw (1442 rmd and 1444 shw only partly) → The list leaves out three largely filled tables: 1438shw, 1439rmd and 1443rmd (fully filled). 1444zhj is an unfilled 118-row template. 1440 has no country table in any month.
- For 1445-1448, the pages checked show a 118-row template with '????' in nearly every date cell → From 1444zhj on, every country table is an empty 118-row template, with 0 cells filled rather than 'nearly every' cell unfilled. 1445rmd and 1445shw have no table. 1448rmd carries a 1447 title. The last filled data is 1444shw (partial).
- Older pages (e.g. 1430rmd) are long prose with country announcements inside the sighting reports and no table → At least 1430rmd has a per-country first-day list grouped by date (list markup, not a table), so older years are also covered in structured form.
