# Egypt

> Research notes for the Swift Islamic calendar rules. Every statement says whether its source is **official** (the authority's own site or document), **other** (a secondary source) or **none** (not found). Collected 2026-11-05 by web research that identified itself honestly and respected robots.txt; no data was taken from ICOP (astronomycenter.net) or moonsighting.com.

Egypt. The month start is announced by Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyya (dar-alifta.org) after a sighting on the evening of the 29th of every Hijri month. The sighting is done by joint committees across Egypt. Each committee has a Dar al-Ifta scholar, an astronomer from NRIAG (المعهد القومي للبحوث الفلكية والجيوفيزيقية) and a member of the Egyptian General Authority for Survey (ESA, هيئة المساحة). Calculation is used only to rule a sighting out ("الحساب الفلكي ينفي ولا يثبت"). It cannot establish a month on its own. Dar al-Ifta's current published method (fatwa 3420, 2016-06-16) accepts a sighting only when the moon sets after the sun and the moonset is at least 15 h 40 min after conjunction. It explicitly does not adopt a requirement that the moon stay more than 16 or 20 minutes after sunset. An earlier fatwa (2345, 2011) and Academy of Islamic Research decision 42 (1991) speak of a lag of 10 minutes or more, plus at least 8 degrees from the conjunction line (2011). No official source found states a 5-minute rule. ESA is described as the body officially responsible for issuing the printed calendar (Youm7, secondary). Its website shows a computed Hijri date column, but its rule is not published. For Dhu al-Hijjah, Dar al-Ifta often cites the Saudi Supreme Court decision.

## Criterion

**In use today** (official): الحالة الخامسة: أن يغيب القمر بعد غروب الشمس، بشرط أن يكون هذا المغيب بعد 15 ساعة و40 دقيقة أو أكثر من افتراق الشمس مع القمر، فإننا نثبت -إذا ما رؤي الهلال حينئذٍ- ولادة الشهر. ... أن الحساب الفلكي القطعي ... ينفي ولا يثبت

*English:* Cases 1-4 (moon sets before the sun; with the sun; after the sun but before conjunction; after the sun but less than 15 h 40 min after conjunction): the month cannot be established and any sighting claim is rejected. Case 5: the moon sets after the sun AND 15 h 40 min or more after conjunction. If the crescent is then actually seen, the month is established. Calculation only rules out; sighting establishes. Fatwa 3420, 2016-06-16, Mufti Shawki Allam.

**Since:** not found. Fatwa 3420 is dated 2016-06-16 and describes the current Dar al-Ifta method, but no adoption date is given. It also says that 'for decades' calculation and actual sighting in Egypt have not disagreed.  
**Source:** <https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/Fatwa/Details/12825>
