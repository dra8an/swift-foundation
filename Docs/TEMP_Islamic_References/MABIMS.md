# MABIMS (Brunei, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore)

> Research notes for the Swift Islamic calendar rules. Every statement says whether its source is **official** (the authority's own site or document), **other** (a secondary source) or **none** (not found). Collected 2026-11-05 by web research that identified itself honestly and respected robots.txt; no data was taken from ICOP (astronomycenter.net) or moonsighting.com.

MABIMS: the crescent criterion agreed by the ministers of religion of Brunei Darussalam, Indonesia, Malaysia and Singapore. It is also called "kriteria baru MABIMS", "Neo Visibilitas Hilal MABIMS" or "KIR 2021": at sunset the moon is at least 3° high and at least 6.4° from the sun. The four countries apply it differently. Malaysia uses its westernmost reference point, sighting reports override the criterion, and JAKIM publishes a takwim. Indonesia treats the whole national territory as one area and holds a sidang isbat. Singapore uses calculation only, at Tuas. Brunei uses sighting only for Ramadan, Syawal and Zulhijjah.

## Criterion

**In use today** (official): Singapore, MUIS Office of the Mufti, Mar 2026 (official): "Kriteria Imkanur Ru'yah yang digunapakai sekarang ini menetapkan bahawa (i) ketinggian anak bulan perlu sekurang-kurangnya 3 darjah dan (ii) jarak lengkung antara matahari dan bulan perlu sekurang-kurangnya 6.4 darjah ketika matahari terbenam." \| Malaysia, footer of the JAKIM takwim 2022 and 2025-2027 editions (official): "Penentuan awal bulan hijriah adalah mengikut kriteria kebolehnampakan hilal Imkanur-rukyah. (3 darjah tinggi hilal dan 6.4 darjah elongasi) *1 Muharam 1443H / 10 Ogos 2021" \| Indonesia, PMA No. 1 Tahun 2026 Pasal 4(4), read via pasal.id because peraturan.go.id was unreachable (other): "tinggi bulan Toposentrik 3˚ (tiga derajat)" and "Elongasi bulan Geosentrik 6,4˚ (enam koma empat derajat)" \| Brunei: no official text found.

*English:* Singapore: the criterion now in use requires (i) a crescent altitude of at least 3 degrees and (ii) a sun-moon arc of at least 6.4 degrees, at sunset. Malaysia: the start of each Hijri month follows the imkanur-rukyah visibility criterion (3° altitude, 6.4° elongation), in force from 1 Muharram 1443H = 2021-08-10. Indonesia (regulation of 15 Jan 2026, promulgated 27 Jan 2026): topocentric moon altitude 3°, geocentric elongation 6.4°. Pasal 4(3) says the thresholds follow the agreement of the ministers of religion of the four countries.

**Since:** Each country started on a different date. Malaysia: 1 Muharram 1443H = 2021-08-10 per the JAKIM takwim footer (official); the Perak state mufti also says from Muharram 1443H (official). RTM, a government broadcaster (other), reports approval at the 257th Conference of Rulers on 24-25 Nov 2021 and says "berkuat kuasa sejak 1443H (2022)". Indonesia: decided 8 Dec 2021 and first applied at the start of Ramadan 1443H/2022 (sulteng.kemenag.go.id, official); written into PMA 1/2026 on 15 Jan 2026. Singapore: used the 1992 criterion "hingga tahun 2021" and the new one afterwards (MUIS, official); MUIS 2022 says the criterion was "finally revised in 2021". Brunei: adoption date not found.

**Coverage.** Answer to the main question: the 3°/6.4° values are right, but Banda Aceh for all four countries is not. Each country tests in its own territory: Malaysia at its westernmost point (Tanjung Chinchin up to 1443H, Pulau Perak from 1444H) with national sighting overriding; Indonesia anywhere in NKRI (in practice Sabang/Banda Aceh), with topocentric altitude and GEOCENTRIC elongation set by PMA 1/2026; Singapore at Tuas by calculation only; Brunei by sighting only for Ramadan, Syawal and Zulhijjah, with istikmal. A month never exceeds 30 days.  Data coverage, AH 1440-1460: - Malaysia (JAKIM takwim, official): continuous from 1440/1 (2018-09-11) to 1450/8 (2028-12-18). Each takwim edition joins the next one without a gap or overlap. The only revised edition is the Syawal 1443 update. The 2025-2027 editions are forecasts made in advance, signed for example 11/23 for 2025. For 1446 and 1447, Ramadan, Syawal and Zulhijjah were checked against announcements and match. For 1440-1445 I did not check each announcement separately. - Indonesia: complete months only for 1445/7 to 1449/8, from the KHI 2024-2027 PDFs. For 1440-1444 only Ramadan, Syawal and Zulhijjah, from isbat releases. The KHI 2019-2023 PDFs were unreachable because of site maintenance. Several isbat dates were confirmed from kemenag.go.id search-result snippets rather than full page reads; Ramadan 1446, Syawal 1447 and Zulhijjah 1447 were read in full. - Singapore: complete months for 2024 and 2025 from MUIS calendars; otherwise only announcements, for 1441/10, 1443/10, 1443/12, 1444/10, 1444/12 and 1446-1447. - Brunei: only a few dates, all from secondary sources. - Nothing is published beyond 1450/8 by JAKIM or 1449/8 by Kemenag.  How the dates were extracted: - Text PDFs (JAKIM 2018-2020 and 2028; Kemenag 2025-2027): parsed from word coordinates with my own scripts. Kemenag 2025 and 2027 months the parser flagged were checked against the page images. - Scanned or vector PDFs (JAKIM 2021-2027; Kemenag 2024): read by eye from rendered images. Transcription errors are possible, although continuity checks across editions passed.
